No deviation from the configuration distributed with ichorCNA. Every argument
above is either a run_ichorCNA() default or a value distributed with the package.

Revised 2026-09-11 (v2.1). --normal previously carried the extra starting point
0.99. It was removed: across 11 flank sizes and 60 development samples the two
grids give Spearman correlations against lcWGS identical to four decimals at
flanks 20-100, and nine of the ten sample cases are unchanged to five decimals.
The grid only seeds the EM restarts; the likelihood picks the winner.

Revised 2026-09-10. --txnE, --txnStrength and --normal2IgnoreSC are now the
defaults of ichorCNA::run_ichorCNA() / getDefaultParameters() themselves. The
values used previously (0.9999 / 1e4, and normal2IgnoreSC left at the wrapper's
1.0) came from ichorCNA's snakemake config, giving a transition prior a thousand
times weaker than the package intends and leaving subclonal states active on the
high-normal EM restarts. On ten healthy-donor blanks that raised the limit of
blank to 4.28%, above the 3% clinical threshold; restored, it is 1.74%.
These are package defaults, not values fitted to this data.

The lcWGS reference arm is run WITHOUT --normalPanel (ichorCNA default for that assay).
